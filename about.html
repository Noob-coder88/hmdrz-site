> So, about me...

My name is HamidReza. I'm 17, and I study computer science in school.

I like going out and hanging out with my friends.

In my free time—which is kind of my whole life—I like watching shows more than anything else. I also like listening to music, playing chess, and learning another language. That's pretty much it.

You might be wondering why I'm so obsessed with shows. Well, they mean a lot to me. I've grown up with them, lived with them, and in some ways, lived through them.
I've experienced and learned things from them that I didn't—or couldn't—experience in my own life.
They've helped shape me into the person I am today.

Music is also an important part of my life. It's not a lie when I say I like almost everything when it comes to music, but what I listen to most is rock and metal.

As for chess, I just like it, even though I'm not very good at it.

And the night... I just love it. The silence, the loneliness, the darkness—everything about it.

That's it, I guess. I don't want to write too much and overshare like I usually do.
But if you've somehow made it all the way here, thanks for reading.
